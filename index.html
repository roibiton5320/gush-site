Installing takes two minutes

We're not in Apple's paid signing program yet, so your Mac asks you to approve the installer once. That's the only extra step.

  1. Open the fileDouble-click the .pkg you downloaded, in your Downloads folder. Both plugins install the same way.
  2. Press DoneYour Mac says it can't check the file. Press Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. Open AnywaySystem Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, press Open Anyway, enter your password, and run it to the end.
  4. Rescan in your DAWAbleton: Settings → Plug-Ins → Rescan. Logic and GarageBand pick up the AU on their own.

Each one also installs a standalone app, so you can try it without a DAW. Stuck? Tell us where in the feedback form and leave your email; we'll write back.
